THOMÄ & BALTHES: OUR ROSELN FAMILY  -  reunion edition
=====================================================

For the Balthes-Thomä family reunion (2027). Built from the same sources as Roseln Families and Roseln Village
(ROSELN_REUNION=1), using the Roseln and Magarei church books, Christian Weiss, Roseln mitten in Siebenbürgen (2010),
family photographs, headstone photographs (Randy Harris, 2016 and 2018) and the film of Johann Balthes and Sofia Thome.
No family-tree (GED) data. People: the thirteen children of Johann Balthes & Sofia Thome and of Michael Thome, all
their ancestors, the ancestors' brothers and sisters and the husbands and wives; others they mention are named only.

Pages: index.html (English, home), familien.html (German), map.html and karte.html (maps). The Family tab has the
branch picker, the family story, Did you know?, family names, family houses and the reunion quiz.

This folder IS the website. Keep it private until Weiss and Albrich have agreed to publication. Opening index.html
straight from the computer does not work; use the web address, or run "python -m http.server" here and open
http://localhost:8000 .
